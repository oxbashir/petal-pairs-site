Petal Pairs privacy policy

Last updated 28 September 2026.

Petal Pairs is a memory game for young children. A child can play it without an account. The app does not ask for a name, email, photo, or location.

What stays on the device

Stars, finished levels, and the sound on/off choice are saved on the device. That information is not sent to the developer.

Advertising

The free app shows ads from Google AdMob so it can stay free. Ad requests are marked for child-directed treatment, limited to a general-audience rating, muted, and not personalized. The Android advertising ID is not used. Google may process the technical data it needs to deliver and measure an ad under its rules for child-directed apps.

Ad earnings are paid to the developer through Google AdMob. The app has no account balance of its own.
